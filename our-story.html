Twenty years spent getting one sauce right

M’Dee started in 2006 as a hobby that got out of hand. Mohd Rosidi Abdul Rashid was teaching computer science in Bukit Mertajam and cooking at home in the evenings. He kept adjusting one recipe, the sauce for char koay teow, until friends stopped asking for the recipe and started asking him to bottle it. He made five hundred bottles. They sold.
‍
He took early retirement from teaching and moved production into Kompleks Perda Food Park in Tasek Gelugor. The recipe has not changed since. The kitchen now turns out thousands of bottles a month, holds Halal certification from JAKIM and MeSTI from the Ministry of Health, and reaches homes through Mydin, Sunshine, Agrobazaar, Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop.

Our mission is to hand every home cook the Penang hawker taste, without the twenty years of practice behind it

A proper char koay teow lives or dies on the sauce, and that is the part that takes decades to balance. We have done it for you. You bring the prawns, the egg, the kuey teow and a hot wok, and dinner tastes like the stall you grew up with.
