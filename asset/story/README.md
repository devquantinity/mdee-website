# Shop hero and story photography

Web-sourced illustrative photography for the shop hero and the “Hawker taste,
home kitchen” collage in `home-shop.html`. These are serving and ingredient
photographs, not photographs of M’Dee staff, premises, or documented M’Dee dishes.
Downloaded on 2026-09-27 and stored locally so the section does not depend on
third-party image hosting. Public photo credits are available below the collage.
